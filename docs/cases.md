# Worked Example

## AI assistance in maintenance documentation

**Status:** Hypothetical research design.

A software team releases a demo of an AI assistant drafting a maintenance note from source documents. One narrative is that source-linked drafts could cut writing time while preserving review quality. An alternative is that the demo works on unusually clean inputs and generates interest because it is new.

A team can test the claim with representative, de-identified tasks. Assign comparable cases to the existing process and the assisted process. Measure total completion time including corrections, and have reviewers who do not know which process was used check for missing or unsupported statements.

| Stage | Example observation |
| --- | --- |
| Information | Release notes and a source-linked demonstration become available |
| Narrative | The audience encounters the claim that the tool reduces drafting time while preserving review quality |
| Attention | Relevant teams view the demo or request details |
| Expectations | Teams estimate time savings before and after seeing the demo |
| Capital | A team approves staff time or funding for a pilot |
| Behavior | Staff complete tasks in the pilot and continue using the workflow after the initial trial |

If interest rises while completion time and review quality remain similar, the observations support increased attention and leave the productivity claim unresolved. If assisted tasks are faster and review quality meets a pre-specified threshold, the result supports the claim for the tasks tested.

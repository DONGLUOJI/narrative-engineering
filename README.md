# Narrative Engineering | 叙事工程学

Narrative Engineering studies how information becomes narrative, how narrative shapes attention and expectations, and how expectations interact with capital and behavior.

**Information → Narrative → Attention → Expectations → Capital → Behavior**

Developed by [DONGLUOJI | 董逻辑](https://github.com/DONGLUOJI). These pages document a working research framework and its application to markets, business, personal brands, and AI systems.

- [Core theory](docs/theory.md) defines each stage and its observable signals.
- [Research methods](docs/methods.md) describes how to identify, construct, and test a narrative.
- [Worked example](docs/cases.md) applies the method to a hypothetical AI product launch.
- [Market narrative validation](docs/market-narrative-validation.md) applies the framework to investment research and includes a reusable research card.
